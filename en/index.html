About the artist

Ivailo Nikolov (b. 1982) is an accomplished artist with exceptional talent and a distinctive voice. Each new subject seems to dictate its own style and approach — his paintings are alive with color, light, texture, atmosphere and energy.

A professional artist for over 15 years, he graduated from the Art College in Varna and later studied at the Art Academy in Veliko Tarnovo. He also spent four years working in Italy at the invitation of renowned artist Athos Faccincani.

Landscape painting is his true passion — inspired by beautiful, memorable places, he continues to travel the world in search of new perspectives and light. His paintings are held in private and corporate collections, as well as galleries internationally.
